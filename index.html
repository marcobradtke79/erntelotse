Erntelotse – plan your garden, bed by bed and week by week

Erntelotse is a free planner for your kitchen garden. You draw your garden on a map, decide what grows where and when, and the app tells you what to do at the right time: sowing, planting, fertilizing, pruning, watering and harvesting.

It keeps a seed stock with a shopping list, warns about poor crop rotation and bad neighbours, works offline and needs no account. Available in English, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese.
